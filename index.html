Scoring Points = base × (1 + Bonus) × Mult. “+%” cards and combo add to Bonus; the table multiplier and big moments multiply. Mult above ×20 is damped (Overload).

Hits Clean hits trigger your cards. Glancing touches score 50% and quick re-hits of the same target 25%, and neither triggers anything. Card effects never trigger other counters, and each effect has a rate limit.

On touch screens, tap the left or right half of the table. Hold Launch to plunge.
